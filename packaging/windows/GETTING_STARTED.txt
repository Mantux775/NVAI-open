NVAI-open for Windows - Getting Started
===================================

NVAI-open generates images, audio and video locally on your graphics card
(NVIDIA; AMD Radeon experimental).
You use it in your browser at http://127.0.0.1:8100.

Requirements
------------
- Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) or Windows 11, 64-bit
- NVIDIA graphics card, 12 GB of video memory or more recommended, with a
  current driver (https://www.nvidia.com/drivers). The setup checks this.
- or an AMD Radeon RX 6000, 7000 or 9000 (experimental) with a current driver
  (AMD Software: Adrenalin Edition). 3D models need an NVIDIA card.
- Internet access during setup (about 3 GB) and for each model (5 to 30 GB)
- Free disk space: about 10 GB for the program, plus room for the models

When you first run the setup
----------------------------
Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". The setup is not signed
(yet). Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

After copying its files, the setup installs Python, ComfyUI and PyTorch. A
console window stays open while this happens and closes by itself at the
end. This takes 5 to 20 minutes.

First steps
-----------
1. Open "NVAI-open" from the Start menu. Your browser shows the interface.
2. Under "Models", download an image model. NVAI-open suggests models that suit
   your graphics card.
3. Under "Generate", enter a prompt. The first job after a start takes an
   extra 30 to 60 seconds because the model is being loaded.

"Quit NVAI-open" in the Start menu shuts everything down and frees the graphics
card.

FLUX 3 and LTX-2.5: Hugging Face account
----------------------------------------
FLUX 3 Image Klein (images) and LTX-2.5 (video with sound) are behind a license
confirmation on Hugging Face. For them you need a free account at
https://huggingface.co:
1. Signed in, accept the license on the model page:
   https://huggingface.co/black-forest-labs/flux-3-image-klein-comfy
   https://huggingface.co/Lightricks/LTX-2.5
2. Create a token (Read) at https://huggingface.co/settings/tokens
3. Enter the token in NVAI-open under "Models", in the "Hugging Face" box.
The model card then shows whether access works. Illustrated guide:
https://github.com/Mantux775/NVAI-open/blob/main/docs/HUGGINGFACE.md
Licenses: FLUX 3 commercial use only below US$5 million annual revenue, review
every image before publishing it; LTX-2.5 free below US$10 million annual revenue.

AMD Radeon (experimental)
-------------------------
The setup installs PyTorch 2.13 with ROCm 10 (AMD's packages for Windows). If the
card does not compute with it, it uses ROCm 7.2.1 instead. Under "Models", the
filter "For your GPU" shows the matching versions (FP8 or bf16 instead of NVFP4,
which only RTX 50 computes in hardware). Little is measured on AMD yet: feedback
via "Report a problem" (top right in NVAI-open) helps.

3D models (if installed)
------------------------
In NVAI-open under "Models", download TripoSG (7.4 GB). Then turn any generated image
into a 3D mesh (GLB). Best results: one object, plain background, no cast shadow.

PhotoCraft add-on (if installed)
--------------------------------
Start menu: "NVAI-open for PhotoCraft". It requires PhotoCraft itself:
https://github.com/storytold/photocraft/releases/latest (version 0.10.0 or newer)

If something goes wrong
-----------------------
Please send these files along with your report:
- %LOCALAPPDATA%\NV-AI\install.log     (setup)
- %LOCALAPPDATA%\NV-AI\nvai-server.log (server)
- %LOCALAPPDATA%\NV-AI\comfyui.log     (generation)
(If you chose a different data folder, they are in that folder.)

"Repair NVAI-open" in the Start menu runs the installation steps again.

Uninstalling
------------
Via Settings > Apps > NVAI-open. Models and images are kept unless you choose to
delete them during uninstallation.
